About Me

My name is Ronaldas Paulius Lencevičius (though I go by Ronas) and I am working on my PhD in the Department of Statistics & Applied Probability (PSTAT) @ University of California, Santa Barbara. Previously, I completed a double B.S. in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics as well as a M.S. in Mathematics @ California State Polytechnic University, Pomona.

My research interests are statistical & machine learning, model robustness, and computational reproducibility. I am also passionate about software development and enjoy contributing to open source projects. I am currently working as the Research Computing Assistant for PSTAT Computing where I develop reproducible research computing tools used by our department and maintain the PSTAT Computing Wiki. I am also a Graduate Student Mentor for the Pacific Alliance for Low-income Inclusion in Statistics and Data Science (PALiISaDS) program. Previously, I was an Adjunct Officer for the PSTAT Graduate Student Committee. Additionally, I am a certified Carpentries Instructor, collaborating with the UCSB Library's DREAM Lab to co-lead workshops that support research computing skills across disciplines.

Some fun facts: I'm from Lithuania 🇱🇹 and moved to California in 2004. I love exploring and going out to naturey places 🌲 to practice my photography 📷 with my Pentax K-S2. I really enjoy travel 🗺️ whether it is biking 🚲 to my favorite park, driving 🏎️ coast-to-coast, or flying 🛩️ to the opposite side of the world with a handful of coins in my pocket and a dream. I am also rather fond of cats 🐱 and help out at the local cat shelter. Lastly, I am the creator and maintainer of (Unofficial) RuneScape Themes for Visual Studio Code, VSCodium, and Zed.

I am happy to connect and chat about anything at the drop of a hat :^)
